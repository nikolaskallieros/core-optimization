## Upper deck arrangement

The top deck provided shielding between the upper access space and reactor hall and supported the fuel transfer flask. It consisted of two rotating plates within an outer stationary ring. Rotating the plates enabled alignment for shutdown fuel handling with the building crane and shielded flask. The sources do not describe this as on-power refuelling. [CNL 2022, p. 3-5](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146078E.pdf#page=145); [AECL-3523, p. 3](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4114552#page=8).

| Deck layer | Reported thickness | Function described or inferred |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Cast steel | 0.45 m | Main documented plate body. |
| Masonite wood-fibre hardboard | 9 cm | Additional shielding layer; the source does not partition neutron/gamma attenuation by layer. |
| Steel cover plate | 0.5 cm | Cover over the layered assembly. |

Source: [CNL 2022, §3.2.1.2](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146078E.pdf#page=145). The sum of the stated layers is 0.545 m, but it is not a specification of every local opening, overlap or bearing feature.

## Coupled duties

The deck had both shielding and load-bearing duties. Inference: a fuel-handling alignment must preserve the intended shield path while carrying the flask and maintaining access to the selected channel. The public descriptions do not provide bearing loads, overlap geometry, drive details, position sensing or the complete deck/flask interlock logic.

The upper service region also contained hot organic joints. The operating report treated that region as a fire-prevention concern because leakage was more likely around the numerous closures and ventilation/insulation options were limited. Its shielding/access design therefore shared space with coolant containment and local CO₂ protection. [AECL-3523, §5](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4114552#page=11).

## Local radiation evidence

During high-power commissioning, narrow beams up to 300 mR/h were found at the bottoms of ion-chamber housings. The detector output itself was also higher than predicted because the cavity flux gradient differed from the calculation. These are separate radiation-protection and instrument-response consequences of local geometry. The retrieved account does not document the final as-built shielding correction. [AECL-2552, §§4–5](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4513855#page=9).

## Remaining information

Opening sizes, labyrinth details, shield-plug arrangements at every penetration, deck diameter, rotation drives, structural load cases and dose fields in each handling configuration were not recovered. The existence of thick deck plates cannot establish the field at an open or misaligned penetration. Conversely, a local startup streaming observation cannot be generalized to the entire reactor hall or all later operating configurations.
