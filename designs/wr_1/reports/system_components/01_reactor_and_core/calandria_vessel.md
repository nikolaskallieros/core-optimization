## Function and boundaries

The calandria contained the heavy-water moderator and reflector surrounding the fuel channels. The hot organic coolant remained inside individual pressure tubes. The vessel therefore performed moderator containment and structural functions while also forming part of the moderator-dump shutdown arrangement. Its overall height is not the active fuel length. [CNL 2020, §2.2](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146112E.pdf#page=8).

| Component or region | Documented role |
| --- | --- |
| Stainless-steel shell | Cylindrical vessel with a torispherical upper head and dished lower head. |
| Upper core space | Held the moderator/reflector around the fuelled channel region. |
| Dump ring assembly | Divided upper core space from lower dump space. |
| Lower dump space | Normally contained helium and received moderator spillage; accepted moderator during a trip. |
| Connection to moderator dump tank | Drained dumped heavy water to the separate tank in Room 107. |
| Suspension rods | Suspended the calandria from the radial or side thermal shield. |
| Auxiliary penetrations | Included moderator spray nozzles, helium purge lines, and moderator sampling and drain lines. |

These are the later CNL equipment descriptions, whose underlying historical manual revision is not established here. [CNL 2020, §2.2](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146112E.pdf#page=8).

## Normal regulation and shutdown

Helium differential pressure between the upper and lower spaces supported the moderator in the core region. Changing that differential pressure changed moderator level and hence reactivity. Rapid shutdown removed moderator from the fuelled region into the lower space and dump tank. Removing fission power did not eliminate the separate need to cool the fuel. [AECL-3523, p. 1](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4114552#page=6).

Commissioning showed that this arrangement depended on more than valve opening. Excessive moderator level allowed overflow into the helium balance line and impaired the dump rate; a high-calandria-level trip was added. Rapid moderator cooling contracted the gas above it, raised its level and introduced positive reactivity; a moderator/process-water temperature-difference trip was installed. The numerical trip settings were not published in that account. [AECL-2552, §2.1](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4513855#page=6).

## Engineering significance and remaining information

The commissioning observations establish a coupled gas-pressure, free-surface and thermal response. A vessel model that fixes moderator level independently of helium pressure would omit an observed mechanism. This is a qualitative interpretation; the source does not provide the volumes, heat-transfer coefficients or controller characteristics needed to calculate its transient magnitude.

The recovered descriptions do not establish dump-ring dimensions, support loads, shell design pressure, weld details, nozzle reinforcement or the complete dump-area versus level relation. Resolving the diameter discrepancy requires an identified vessel drawing and revision. Neither a cylindrical-volume estimate using one reported diameter nor the whole-system heavy-water inventory can supply the missing core-space volume reliably.
