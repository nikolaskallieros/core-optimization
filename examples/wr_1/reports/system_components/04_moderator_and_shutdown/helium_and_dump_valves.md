## Operating principle

Helium differential pressure between the core space and lower dump space supported the heavy-water level in the core. Two regulating valves controlled this differential pressure. Changing moderator level changed reactivity; opening the dump path allowed rapid removal of moderator from the core. Helium also provided a cover atmosphere that limited entry of air and light-water vapour into the moderator system. [CNL 2020, §§2.6](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146112E.pdf#page=9).

## Commissioning modifications

| Observation | Reported action and significance |
| --- | --- |
| Blower capacity caused pump-up reactivity addition above the 0.33-mk/s design maximum | Speed reduced from 1,200 to 900 rpm. Reduced efficiency then caused pump-up rate to fall as level rose and shifted equilibrium control-valve positions. |
| Moderator overflow entered the helium balance line | High-calandria-level trip added because overflow impaired moderator dumping. |
| Compressed-asbestos gaskets leaked helium | Replaced with Buna-N, which later shed material; Neoprene was then used successfully in the reported period. |
| Rubber particles obstructed the spray-nozzle protective screen | Demonstrated that gas-system sealing materials could affect connected moderator equipment. |

Source: [AECL-2552, §2.1](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4513855#page=6). These are historical modifications, not a recommendation of those gasket materials for a present design.

## Measured shutdown response

The startup table gives a design first-second moderator-dump reactivity change of 12.5 mk and a measured magnitude of 15.5 mk. The context is removal of reactivity through moderator dumping. The table's first-second quantity is not a complete shutdown trajectory, an end-state margin or a time-independent rate applicable to every core. [AECL-2552, Table I](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4513855#page=9).

The same commissioning work used 19- and 25-site cores. Later fuel compositions, loading patterns and helium-system revisions cannot be assumed to retain the same measured value without their own basis. Nor should the six dump valves be interpreted as six wholly independent shutdown systems.

## Interfaces and missing specifications

The helium system connects neutron-power demand to valve action, gas pressure, moderator level and core reactivity. It also exchanges heat and water vapour with the moderator. The rapid-cooling event demonstrates that gas-space thermal contraction could move the liquid level even without a commanded increase in reactivity. [AECL-2552, §2.1](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4513855#page=6).

No full blower curves, accumulator capacity, gas pressures, valve flow coefficients, actuation times, leak-rate criteria, recombiner capacity or logic diagrams were recovered. The equipment inventory supports functional tracing, but reconstructing shutdown performance would require those data and the level-dependent reactivity worth for the specified core. A complete safety case cannot be inferred from the favourable startup first-second measurement alone.
