## Function and equipment

Heavy water moderated and reflected neutrons and removed gamma/thermal heat deposited in the vessel and tubes. It circulated separately from the organic fuel coolant. The moderator level also set reactivity, making circulation and cooling relevant to reactor control as well as structural heat removal. [CNL 2020, §2.6](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146112E.pdf#page=9).

## Dumping and gas separation

When moderator left the core during shutdown, it passed through the calandria dump space toward the external dump tank. Commissioning found that entrained helium could gas-lock the moderator pumps during this operation. The remedy extended the discharge pipe across the tank and added baffles to keep entrained gas away from the pump inlet. [AECL-2552, §2.1](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4513855#page=6).

This observation establishes a hydraulic interface between dump flow and circulation-pump suction. It does not give a gas-separation efficiency, allowable void fraction or pump coastdown curve. Three installed pumps likewise do not establish a particular single-failure capability without their duty ratings and electrical configuration.

## Cooling and reactivity

Rapid moderator cooling contracted the atmosphere above the liquid and raised moderator level, producing excessive positive reactivity insertion. A trip limited the temperature difference between moderator and process water. Independently, measured moderator temperature coefficients changed from +0.135 mk/°C at 10°C to zero at 50°C and −0.09 mk/°C at 80°C in the startup study. The physical level response and the material temperature coefficient are different effects and should not be combined into one constant. [AECL-2552, §2.1 and Table I](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4513855#page=6).

The heat exchanger's cooling action therefore cannot be judged solely by whether it removes more heat. A coupled interpretation needs moderator temperature, helium pressure/volume, level and regulator response. The cited source does not provide enough data to quantify that full dynamic response.

## Moderator purity and maintenance

Construction and commissioning emphasized clean, dry components and exclusion of light water. Equipment factory-tested with light water was checked for remaining moisture; drying and helium purging preceded heavy-water addition. The commissioning account reported no measurable isotopic downgrading and a moderator isotopic content of 99.78%. This is an observed commissioning condition, not a lifetime purity guarantee. [AECL-2552, §2.1](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4513855#page=6).

The collection tank received maintenance drains from moderator pumps, helium purge lines and sample lines; temporary connections could drain other equipment. That collection function is distinct from the moderator dump tank's rapid-shutdown function. [CNL 2020, p. 10](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146112E.pdf#page=10).

Missing information includes pump duties, total heat load, normal moderator temperatures across operating eras, tank volumes, baffle geometry, bellows design and the temperature-difference trip setting. The retrieved evidence supports the process arrangement and observed failure mechanisms, not a complete cooling-system performance calculation.
