WR-1 was refuelled during shutdown. Irradiated fuel initially remained in organic coolant while a shielded flask moved it from the channel to storage. Water outside the organic-filled tubes and cans supplied shielding and removed heat. Fuel-contact fluid and the external cooling/shielding water therefore remained distinct inventories. [CNL 2020, §2.8](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146112E.pdf#page=11).

## Historical sequence

1. After shutdown, use the shielded fuel flask to remove fuel from its channel.
2. Place it in an organic-filled storage tube within the water-filled storage block.
3. After sufficient decay cooling, transfer it through the shielded station into an organic-filled can in the storage bays.
4. Wash and transfer selected fuel to the hot cells for examination, bundle shuffling or disassembly; use the appropriate flask for the load.

This summarizes the historical description, not an operating procedure. CNL reports initial removal at about six hours and a usual two-to-six-week storage-block period, but the controlling later-transfer condition was acceptable sheath temperature during the transfer. The public text does not give that temperature limit. [CNL 2020, §2.8](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146112E.pdf#page=11).

## Important interfaces

| Interface | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Flask to channel and deck | Requires alignment, load support, shielding and coolant containment. |
| Organic-filled tube to surrounding water | Separates fuel chemistry from the external heat sink. |
| Stored fuel to bay circulation | Decay heat remains after removal from the core. |
| Fuel surface to washing system | Removes opaque coolant film and transfers activity to solvent/drainage. |
| Fuel hardware to shuffling tools | Irradiation and hydrogen embrittlement can change handling integrity. |

The last interface is supported by later WR-1 reports of some endplate breakage during the programme; it is not an assumed failure of every fuel string. [AECL-6436, p. 47](https://inis.iaea.org/records/e4zqc-d2129/files/11559681.pdf#page=54).

No complete handling load case, storage criticality calculation, drop analysis, allowable decay-heat schedule or operating interlock specification was recovered. Different flask designs must not be assigned each other's cooling or protection features.
