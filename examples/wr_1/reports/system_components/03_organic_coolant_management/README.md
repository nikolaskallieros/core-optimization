The organic auxiliaries maintained coolant inventory, pressure support and composition while removing gases, selected volatile products, high boilers and fouling material. These functions were related but not interchangeable: a particulate filter could not replace vacuum distillation, and bulk high-boiler concentration alone did not establish low fouling potential. [AECL-2943, §§2.4–2.5](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4557804#page=9).

## Functional connections

| Process | Controlled concern | Important coupling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Degassing and condensation | Dissolved gases and volatile inventory | Vapour pressure and coolant consumption. |
| Inventory/pressurizing equipment | Coolant availability and circulation support | Thermal swell, fuel handling and minor leakage. |
| Vacuum distillation/feed-and-bleed | High-boiler concentration and reusable fractions | Low-temperature viscosity and makeup demand. |
| Clay and backup filtration | Fouling substances and particles | Fuel heat transfer; contamination following failures. |
| Sampling and chemistry adjustment | Water, chlorine, oxygen ingress and composition | Zirconium oxide protection and hydrogen uptake. |

The source descriptions establish these roles; they do not supply a closed plant-wide mass balance. Distinct circuit inventories also allowed coolant comparisons, but shared services and experimental-loop connections limit any claim of complete independence. [CNL, §§2.4–2.5](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/p80124/146112E.pdf#page=9).

HB-40 began as the startup choice and became the reference coolant by 1967. The earlier plan to replace it with Santowax OM is therefore not an account of a completed plant conversion. Historical coolant-management costs published for a hypothetical 200-MWe station are not WR-1 departmental costs. [AECL-2943, pp. 3–6](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4557804#page=7).

Equipment dimensions are strongest for the later degassing inventory. Process throughput, full valve routing, achieved separation efficiencies and operating setpoints remain incomplete. C-circuit values are retained separately wherever the inventory identifies differences.
