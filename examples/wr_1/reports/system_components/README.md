# WR-1 system and component reports

This collection expands the WR-1 hierarchy into **eight system overviews and 29 dedicated component-group reports**. Each component-group report addresses the individual items named in its hierarchy entry. This folder and its subfolders contain Markdown files only.

| System | Detailed reports | Main subjects |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [1. Reactor and core structures](01_reactor_and_core/README.md) | 4 | Calandria, annuli, pressure channels, closures, fuel and supports. |
| [2. Main heat transport](02_main_heat_transport/README.md) | 3 | Organic circuits, pumps, flow restrictions, connections and water heat rejection. |
| [3. Organic coolant management](03_organic_coolant_management/README.md) | 5 | Pressure/inventory, condensers, distillation, clay/filters, chemistry and supply. |
| [4. Moderator, control and shutdown](04_moderator_and_shutdown/README.md) | 4 | Heavy water, helium, dump equipment, boron and neutron protection. |
| [5. Protection and essential services](05_protection_and_services/README.md) | 4 | Emergency cooling, monitoring, ventilation, fire protection, power and air. |
| [6. Shielding and structural cooling](06_shielding_and_structural_cooling/README.md) | 3 | Thermal shields, biological concrete, cooling, deck plates and penetrations. |
| [7. Fuel handling and examination](07_fuel_handling/README.md) | 3 | Flasks, cranes, organic-filled storage, water cooling, washing and hot cells. |
| [8. Research facilities and waste services](08_research_and_waste/README.md) | 3 | Water/organic test loops, external hydraulics, drainage and liquid waste. |

Each system overview links to all of its component reports. The [overall WR-1 report](../wr1_systems_and_operating_experience.md) provides the complete hierarchy and broader operating context.

The reports distinguish the 1965 design, startup modifications, early operating experience, later UC fuel and circuit-C changes, and retrospective CNL inventories. They retain conflicting dimensions and counts, label engineering inferences, and identify missing specifications. The level of detail follows the available evidence; these are historical source reviews, not operating manuals or reconstructed safety qualifications.
